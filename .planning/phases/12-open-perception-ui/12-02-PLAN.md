<objective>
Build the `openarena_perception/visualization/panels/` subpackage with three self-contained panel classes (MinimapPanel, WireframePanel, StatsPanel), extend `hud.py` with a `decoration_only` mode, and ship panel isolation tests. This plan produces NO executable app — that is Plan 12-03. Each panel is independently testable by calling `panel.draw(state, surface)` on a bare `pygame.Surface` under `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy`.
